FAQ

Common questions.

Is it really free to get started?+
Yes — sign up, create your first tender with AI, and invite vendors at no cost. No credit card required to start.
How long does it take to publish my first RFP?+
Most teams publish their first RFP in under 4 minutes. Paste a paragraph describing what you need, and our AI turns it into a structured brief. Review, adjust, and publish.
Can vendors respond without creating an account?+
Vendors need a free Bid account to submit proposals — this keeps everything structured and verifiable. Registration takes less than 2 minutes.
Is my data secure?+
Bid is SOC 2 Type II certified and PDPL compliant. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never share your vendor relationships or tender data with third parties.
What's a Traction Link?+
A Traction Link is your company's unique vendor-request page (e.g. bidapp.sa/acme-corp). Share it anywhere — your website, email signature, LinkedIn — and vendors request to join your vendors base directly. You approve, categorise, and invite them to future tenders in one click.
