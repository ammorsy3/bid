Project Scope

Description

We are seeking a qualified cybersecurity firm to conduct a comprehensive security audit and penetration testing of our cloud infrastructure hosted on AWS. The engagement includes an assessment of our EC2 instances, S3 bucket configurations, IAM policies, VPC architecture, and all public-facing endpoints. The vendor must deliver a full technical report detailing vulnerabilities discovered, severity classification (CVSS scoring), proof-of-concept for critical findings, and a prioritised remediation roadmap. A follow-up re-test after remediation is required within 30 days of the initial report.

Project Objective

Identify and remediate security vulnerabilities in our AWS cloud infrastructure before the Q3 2026 compliance audit, ensuring we meet ISO 27001 and SAMA CSF requirements.

Deliverables

Address each item clearly in your technical proposal.

1
Executive summary and full penetration testing report with CVSS-scored findings
2

3

4
30-minute debrief call with technical leadership presenting key findings
