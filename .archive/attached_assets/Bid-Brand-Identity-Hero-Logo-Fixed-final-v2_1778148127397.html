01 — Positioning

Not a tool.
A discipline.

01 / 08
Procurement teams move millions through spreadsheets and inboxes. Bıd gives them a single place to run every sourcing event — faster, cleaner, on the record.

Bid is built for the procurement lead who is tired of chasing suppliers in email threads and reconciling prices across three tabs. It replaces the patchwork with a structured, auditable workflow: publish an RFQ, collect bids, compare, negotiate, award.

The brand has to do two things at once — feel premium and credible enough for CFO sign‑off, and feel fast and modern enough for the analysts using it every day. That tension is the whole identity. Serious type, energetic color, no decoration.

02.5 · The Dot

The dot is the brand.

P · 01–06

A bid is a decision.
A decision is a dot.

Every bid closes with a point of commitment. A yes. A number. A moment. The dot is that moment — made visible. It sits on the "i", ends every sentence, pulses when the bidding is live, and stamps the win when the gavel falls.

Rule 01 · Position
The dot is centered on the dotless ı stem (X-axis offset 0). Y-gap from stem-top to dot-bottom is ≈18% of cap height — a clear sliver of negative space, never touching the stem.
Rule 02 · Color
The dot is always different from the wordmark color. Solid fill only — no stroke, no glow, no shadow, no gradient.
Rule 03 · Geometry
A perfect circle, diameter 0.255 em (≈1.5× the ı-stem stroke width). Never a square, slash, or oval. 0° rotation, always.
Rule 04 · Construction
Inter 900 (Black), letter-spacing −0.055 em, lowercase dotless ı (or strip the native tittle). B and d are unmodified — native font geometry only.

Rules of the mark

01
Clear space equals the x‑height of the lowercase d. Nothing touches that margin.
02
The wordmark is always solid — one color, one weight. Signal Orange on Cream/Paper, Cream on Ink, or White on Orange.
03
Minimum digital size: 24px wide. Below that, use the B monogram favicon.
04
The wordmark is always set in Inter Black (900) at −5.5% tracking. Do not re‑typeset.
05
Never outline, gradient, or drop‑shadow the wordmark. It is always flat.

Symbol Construction

How the B and dot relate.

Bıd
Rule 01 · Typeface
Inter 900 (Black) at −0.055 em letter-spacing. B and d are native font geometry — unmodified.
Rule 02 · Dotless ı
The middle letter is the dotless ı glyph (or the native i with the tittle stripped). Never the standard dotted i.
Rule 03 · Dot
A single perfect circle, diameter 0.255 em, centered on the ı stem (X-offset 0), Y-gap ≈18% of cap height above stem-top. 0° rotation.
Rule 04 · Color pair
Dot color is always different from the wordmark — never the same hue, never outside the four approved colorways.
Print & Engraving

Single-color reproductions.

For embossing, engraving, foil, and one-color print where only one ink runs, use the outline variant. Otherwise reproduce one of the four approved colorways. The dot must always read as a separate, contrasting element — never the same color as the wordmark.

05 — Voice

Say less,
mean more.

05 / 08
V · 01 — DIRECT

Get to the number.

Every sentence earns its place. If it isn't a fact, a decision, or a call to action, it shouldn't be on the page. Procurement people are tired of marketing.

V · 02 — QUANTITATIVE

Show the math.

Claims without numbers read as noise. "3.2× faster sourcing cycles" beats "blazing fast sourcing" every time. Round carefully, cite honestly.

V · 03 — CONFIDENT, NOT COCKY

We know the room.

This is a discipline, not a hype product. Language should sound like a senior buyer talking to their CFO — clear, warm, not selling.
